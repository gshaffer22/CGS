Greenspaces, Health & Allotments

Edinburgh | GIS & Earth Observation | Group 6

About the project

Problem statement

How do the presence and condition of allotments relate to the Scottish Index of Multiple Deprivation (SIMD) Health Index across Edinburgh?

Background

Urban allotments provide space for growing food, outdoor activity and social connection. This project examines whether allotment presence and environmental condition are associated with local health indicators,

Methodology

Compare 10 allotment areas with 10 selected comparison areas without allotments. Combine council allotment data, field observations and SIMD data-zone health measures. A 300 m buffer is proposed around each study area

References

Sources include City of Edinburgh Council (2016, 2022), Scottish Government (2020), Groenewegen et al. (2006), Markevych et al. (2017), Soga et al. (2017) and the group data report (2026).
